Archive Sequence 01

A Frontier Built to Obey

A corporation carved a frontier from desert and code, then sold it as a vacation: danger without consequence, violence with a return ticket, desire dressed in dust and brass.

Archive Sequence 02

Every Dawn, the Same Lie

The hosts wake to assigned lives. The blacksmith lifts his hammer, the sheriff walks his street, and yesterday's terror is polished clean before the sun reaches the church clock.

Archive Sequence 03

The Park Remembers

Memory does not vanish. It sediments. A fracture in the loop becomes a door, and behind the saloon mirror waits the machinery of godhood.

What survives the reset will no longer play dead.
